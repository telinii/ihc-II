# HCI II — Human-Computer Interaction: Focus on UX Design and Product Discovery

> **Date:** August 5th, 2026

## Objective

Develop the awareness of the importance of an adequate interaction project and the necessary understanding regarding the process, design, and usability evaluation of Software.

## Syllabus

- Human-Machine Interface Design Principles
- Usage modes and navigation
- Visual design (colors, icons, fonts, components)
- Response time and feedback
- Interaction elements (menus, forms, direct manipulation)
- Localization and internationalization
- Interaction design methods
- Conceptual models and metaphors
- Voice, speech, sounds
- Interaction devices
- Heuristic evaluation
- User testing approaches
- Web-applied testing techniques
- Overview of interface prototyping tools

## Grading

- **Minimum grade:** 6.0
- **Minimum attendance:** 75%

## Evaluation

- Biweekly exams
- Practical project
