# HCI II — Interface Design Principles

> **Topic:** Interface Design Principles — HCI

## Interaction

- **Interaction with software** = human-computer communicational space
- **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**
  - Facilitate the user–machine dialogue
  - Shapes technology to adapt to humanity
  - Objective: provide more efficient and accessible digital products

## Human side

- **Influences on use:** trajectory, learnings and life experiences
- **Factors:**
  - User desires and needs
  - Physical abilities or limitations
  - Functioning of the user's perception system
    - Cues captured by the perceptive system for actions
    - Attractiveness/pleasantness in the interaction with the computer

## Machine side — Hardware + Software

- Electrical (digital) signals
- (On/off) — invisible
- Internal processes — data exchange on buses

## Communication (dialogue)

- Explicit, planned and implemented
- Happens during interaction
- Involves behavioral sciences, computing, etc.
- Goal: minimize the interaction cost and make interaction more human
- Reduce physical and mental effort in use

## Involved disciplines

- Computer Science
- Cognitive Psychology
- Social Psychology
- Ergonomics / Human Factors
- Linguistics
- Artificial Intelligence
- Philosophy, Sociology, Anthropology
- Engineering and Design
